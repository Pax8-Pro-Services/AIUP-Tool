# Pax8 Brand Specification

> Machine-readable brand reference for Pax8 deliverables (presentations, documents, visuals).
> Source: Pax8 2025 Brand Guidelines. Use this as the single source of truth for colors, fonts, and visual rules when generating any Pax8-branded content.

---

## Brand Personality

**Pax8 is a Hero Brand** — bold, ambitious, forward-looking, human. Every deliverable should feel cinematic, modern and purposeful — never a generic corporate template.

| Pax8 IS | Pax8 IS NOT |
|---------|-------------|
| Colorful, Anti-Corporate, Cinematic | Monochromatic, Corporate-looking |
| Surreal, Bold, Modern | Cartoonish, Cliché, Boring |
| Mysterious, Chic | Cute, Uninspired |

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors (use these most)

| Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
|------|-----|-----|------|
| Black | `#000000` | `0, 0, 0` | Default background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | `255, 255, 255` | Primary text color |
| Pax8 Blue | `#0447BF` | `4, 71, 191` | Highlight boxes, key callouts, numbered circles, closing-slide background |

### Secondary Colors (accents only — never dominate)

| Name | Hex | RGB |
|------|-----|-----|
| Green | `#03DE91` | `3, 222, 145` |
| Cyan | `#10D6D3` | `16, 214, 211` |
| Purple | `#67117D` | `103, 17, 125` |
| Pink | `#D7117B` | `215, 17, 123` |
| Yellow | `#EAC106` | `234, 193, 6` |
| Red | `#DF0C13` | `223, 12, 19` |

### Color Usage Rules

- **Black dominates.** Most surfaces use a pure black (`#000000`) background.
- **White + black** carry the majority of visual weight; **Pax8 Blue** is the primary spot color.
- Secondary colors break up blue/black/white combinations — they never dominate.
- A thin green (`#03DE91`) accent line under a subheading is a signature Pax8 pattern (use deliberately, not on every title).
- The "blue box on a key word" — a Pax8 Blue (`#0447BF`) filled rectangle behind an important headline word — is a distinctive Pax8 signature.
- **Never** use muted, pastel, or gray-toned palettes. Pax8 colors are bold and saturated.
- **Never** use a white or light-colored background.
- The signature cover treatment is a rainbow multi-color accent line across the bottom of the title area (Blue → Cyan → Green → Yellow → Red spectrum).

## Typography

### Font Family

**Poppins** is the exclusive Pax8 font. Use nothing else.
(APAC content exception: **Noto Sans**. Code/technical reference: in pptxgenjs use `"Poppins"`; system fallback `"Calibri"`.)

| Weight | Name | Usage |
|--------|------|-------|
| 600 | Poppins SemiBold | Main headlines (H1/H2) |
| 500 | Poppins Medium | Subheadings (H3/H4), section labels |
| 400 | Poppins Regular | Body copy, captions |
| 300 | Poppins Light | Supporting text, fine print |

### Type Hierarchy (slide scale)

| Element | Size | Weight | Color |
|---------|------|--------|-------|
| Slide title / H1 | 40–54pt | SemiBold 600 | White |
| Section header / H2 | 28–36pt | SemiBold 600 | White |
| Subhead / H3 | 20–24pt | Medium 500 | White |
| Body text | 14–16pt | Regular 400 | White |
| Labels / captions | 10–12pt | Regular 400 | White or muted |
| Big data callout | 60–80pt | SemiBold 600 | White or Pax8 Blue |

### Typography Rules

- **Title Case** for all headlines and subheadings (capitalize each major word).
- **Sentence case** for all body copy.
- **No Oxford comma** — anywhere.
- Size contrast between title and body must be obvious — headlines never feel timid.
- Make data callouts large and dramatic.

## Layout & Structure

### Backgrounds

- **Cover slide**: Pure black, white text, signature rainbow accent line across the bottom of the title area.
- **Content slides**: Black background, white text, Pax8 Blue for key callout boxes / highlighted terms.
- **Closing / Thank You**: Deep Pax8 Blue (`#0447BF`) background, white text, cinematic imagery on the right.
- Dark throughout — the generic light/dark "sandwich" approach does **not** apply.

### Spacing

- Minimum **0.4"** margins from all edges.
- **0.3–0.5"** between content blocks.
- Leave breathing room — generous negative space on black is more powerful than a packed slide.
- Titles sit upper-left or upper-center, never cramped.

### Layout Patterns (vary them — don't repeat one on every slide)

1. **Statement Slide** — large bold quote/statement, minimal else. The text IS the design (48–60pt SemiBold White).
2. **Two-Column Split** — text/headline left, image/stat/visual right. Blue box can highlight the key headline word.
3. **Numbered List with Circles** — colored circles (Pax8 Blue or cycling secondary palette) with number, bold subhead, body.
4. **Grid / Card Layout** — 2–3 column grid, bold header + body per cell, alternating secondary accent borders.
5. **Label + Content** — small Pax8 Blue label rectangle (e.g. "Mission") + separator line + large statement. For definitions, frameworks, values.
6. **Full-Bleed Image + Overlay** — cinematic image fills the slide, high-contrast overlay text, logo watermark upper-right.

## Logo Usage

- Always **white or black** — never colorized.
- Never stretch, rotate, or alter proportions.
- Cloud mark is always a **stroke (outline)**, never filled with color.
- Clear space on all sides equal to the height of the cloud mark.
- Place upper-left or upper-right.
- **Never** place over a face or busy area without a contrasting backing shape.
- If no logo asset is available, use a text watermark "pax8" in an upper corner or note a placeholder in speaker notes.

---

## Brand Voice (for any written copy)

**Sound like:** Human, Motivational, Ambitious, Bold, Inspirational, Confident, Forward-looking, Irreverent, Real.
**Not:** Corporate, Arrogant, Playing-it-safe, Cocky, Reserved, Commonplace.

- Short, punchy headlines over long explanatory titles.
- Active voice; present or future tense.
- "You" and "we" language over passive third-person corporate speak.
- Simplify jargon — if a partner wouldn't immediately get it, rephrase.

---

## Quick Do / Don't

### Do
- Use Poppins at every size.
- Title Case all headlines, sentence case all body.
- Use the Pax8 Blue highlight block behind a key headline word.
- Make big numbers dramatic (60–80pt).
- Keep slides spacious — white space on black is powerful.

### Don't
- No white/light backgrounds.
- No font other than Poppins (or Noto Sans for APAC).
- No Oxford comma.
- No pastel, muted, or gray tones.
- No corporate clichés (clip-art, thumbs-up stock, forced-smile office photos).
- No logo over a face or busy area.
- No slide that's pure bullets on black — every content slide needs a visual or strong typographic treatment.
- No accent line under every title as a substitute for layout thinking.
