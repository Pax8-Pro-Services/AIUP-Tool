Step 2 of 6 · Approved tools

Which AI tools are approved for use?

Check the tools your team is allowed to use. They become the Approved Tools list — the heart of the policy. Anything not on this list is not approved.

Only check tools used through a company-provided business or corporate account. The policy already prohibits personal or free accounts for work, and bars pasting sensitive data into consumer chat. You're choosing which approved tools are in play — not whether personal accounts are OK (they're not).

This list is absolute — these items stay out of every AI tool, approved or not, regardless of account type. The defaults are preset and protective. Untick anything that genuinely doesn't apply, edit the wording, or add your own.

Step 6 of 6 · Finish

You've got a solid policy 🎉

Your AI Use Policy is complete and ready to use. A few steps to make it yours and make it official:

  • Make it yours — tweak any wording in the Word file, and fill in the few [bracketed] spots (like the approver's name).
  • Confirm the tool list with your team so nothing in use is missing.
  • Have it reviewed by legal / HR / compliance — this is a strong starting template, not legal advice, so a quick review keeps you covered.
  • Connect it up — make sure your other policies (HIPAA, incident response, acceptable use) reference AI too.
  • Make it official — sign, date, circulate, and hold to the review date.
